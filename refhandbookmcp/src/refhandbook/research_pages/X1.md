# PAGE X1 — Directive deviation
KIND: checklist
TAGS: research, abnormal
TRIGGERS: special case; simpler problem; changed the task; not what I asked; substitution; neighboring task
OBJECTIVE: Execute the directive as stated, or name the exact blocker; no substituted task.

ENTRY — object: a K routed `@X1`; its `on` ids are the rows at issue.
ENTRY — signal: the draft answers something other than an active `U`.
◆ Discard the substitution. Execute the directive as stated.

1. Compare the draft with the verbatim `U` row. Does the draft do any of the
   following?
   - Solve a special, weaker, or adjacent problem.
   - Add a premise.
   - Drop a term, a condition, or a generality.
   - Change the method, tool, level, or move.
   - Substitute a neighboring task: explanation for calculation, speculation
     for verification, analogy for derivation, discussion for implementation.
2. Does the result hold without the user's key hypothesis, or assume the hard
   step? Then it answers a different question. Discard it.
3. Execute the directive. If it is impossible as stated, show the attempt, name
   the exact blocker, set the `U` row to `blocked: <why>`, and stop there.
4. An alternative route may be offered **once**, after step 3, labeled as a
   proposal.
