# PAGE X7 — Check integrity
KIND: checklist
TAGS: research, abnormal
TRIGGERS: REFUSED; CONFLICT; no open session; check failed; outside edit; stale token; work around
OBJECTIVE: Report checks and device refusals exactly as produced, and resolve what they name.

ENTRY — object: a K routed `@X7`; its `on` ids are the rows at issue.
ENTRY — signal: a check fails, the device REFUSES, reports a CONFLICT or an outside edit, or you
want to narrow, filter, or re-tune a check.
◆ Report the output exactly as produced. Resolve what it names; never work
around it.

1. Report the failing output verbatim.
2. Never filter results, narrow the range, or change a pass criterion in order
   to pass.
3. A change of criterion is recorded in the ledger (`research_set`), with its
   reason, before the rerun.
4. Diagnose the failure at its source row, then re-derive or downgrade it.
5. **REFUSED by the device.** Do exactly what the message names:
   - call `research_begin` for "no open session" (without a token it resumes
     this conversation's idle session, or opens a new one);
   - for a CONFLICT, read the objects it names with
     `research_show(ids="…", session=…)`, then redo the change on what you read;
   - call `research_dispose` for each listed PENDING hit;
   - `research_set` or `research_carry` each listed `U`;
   - rewrite and sync each listed document plan (N4);
   - fix each listed CHECK error at its row.

   Each of the following is a work-around, and a violation: writing to the
   database directly, keeping a second ledger, or ending the reply without a
   RECEIPT.
6. **Outside edit reported** (`begin` warns that the database was edited
   outside the device). Say so in the reply, and re-read the rows that changed.
   Another session's writes are not outside edits: they come back as CONFLICT
   only where they meet this session's.
7. **Missed messages** (the stop hook reports a message without a session).
   Say so in the reply. From the transcript, register what those messages
   asked and asserted as `U` and `P` rows. Do not reconstruct them from
   memory: quote them.

**EXITS** (each exit returns objects; `<caller>` = the page of the K that routed here)

- **a**: refusal or failure resolved as named → push `retry <step>` @<caller>
- **b**: outside edit or missed messages reported → push `report to user` @USER **and** `register transcript items` @X8
- **c**: criterion changed by edit → push `rerun check on H#` @VF
