# PAGE VF-CHAIN — Verify granularity: derivation chain or batch
KIND: reference
TAGS: research, reference
TRIGGERS: derivation chain; derivation step; dependent steps; one step per message; step by step; basis tag per line; wait for confirmation; batch of independent claims
OBJECTIVE: How finely claims are checked: a derivation chain one confirmed step per message, independent claims together in one batch.

Consulted from VF step 3 (or any card that checks a derivation); return to that step. Not a procedure: it consumes no K.

- **Derivation chain** (dependent steps):
  1. Take one step per message.
  2. Give each line a basis tag, naming the term or operation it comes from.
  3. Show the step's own check (signs, indices, units, substitution) before its
     result.
  4. Wait for the user's confirmation before the next step.
- **Independent claims**: may be checked in one batch, each with its own
  executed check and its own verdict.
