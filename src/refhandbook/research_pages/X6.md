# PAGE X6 — Invalidation and correction
KIND: checklist
TAGS: research, abnormal
TRIGGERS: correction; that is wrong; superseded; verdict flipped; assumption changed; PENDING
OBJECTIVE: Apply a correction as a scoped state change and propagate it to closure.

ENTRY — object: a K routed `@X6`; its `on` ids are the rows at issue.
ENTRY — signal: one of the following:
- a row was changed, rejected, weakened, strengthened, or reclassified;
- a verdict flipped;
- the user corrected something.

◆ A correction is a state change, not a remark. **Change exactly what was
corrected, nothing more.** Propagate to closure. Never revive a rejected branch.

1. **Scope the correction.** Quote the part the user rejected, and only that
   part.
   - If the rejected part is only a portion of a row, split the row. The
     rejected portion becomes `superseded→<new>`. The remainder is re-added
     unchanged, citing the old row.
   - Retracting, negating, or reclassifying **anything beyond the quoted
     part** is a new claim. It needs an executed comparison or check attached
     (SKILL.md §4). Without one it is not made.
   - `set` the rejected part to `superseded→<new>` or `refuted`, and `add` the
     replacement.
2. The write in step 1 opens PENDING hits (the device prints them). A hit is a
   row, or a verdict cell `H#@config`. For every hit, call
   `research_dispose(hit, of=<id>, disposition=V, session)`, where V is one of:
   - **VALID**: re-derived in this session (a new row, or `set`); the device refuses
     otherwise;
   - **RECHECK**: the device marks it stale and opens the next PENDING layer,
     so the closure continues by itself. A verdict cell goes stale alone, and
     the claim's other configurations keep their verdicts. A premise that was
     on leaves the main line until it is re-derived, and the device opens every
     main verdict cell;
   - **INVALID**: `set` the hit to false, superseded, or refuted first;
   - **UNAFFECTED**: history only; `--why` is required.
3. A deliverable that cites a changed id is not patched. It gets a change
   list and whole-section rewrites (card N4); `research_begin` lists it until
   it is synced.
4. Report every hit with its disposition, unfiltered. The Δ printed by `end`
   lists them.
5. The session cannot `end` while any hit is PENDING or the check shows an
   error.
6. A rejected branch comes back only with new evidence or at the user's
   explicit request.
7. **Reverting a wrong correction.** If a correction itself was wrong:
   - `set` the wrongly superseding row to `refuted`;
   - restore the original row's status;
   - dispose the PENDING hits of both rows.

**EXITS** (each exit returns objects; `<caller>` = the page of the K that routed here)

- **a**: every hit disposed, check 0 → push `resume <caller step>` @<caller>, or `await reaction` @USER when the correction came from the user
- **b**: the correction itself was wrong → push `revert <row>` @X6
