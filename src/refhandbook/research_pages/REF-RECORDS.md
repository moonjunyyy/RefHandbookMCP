# PAGE REF-RECORDS — Keeping records current
KIND: reference
TAGS: research, reference, device
TRIGGERS: keeping records current; record TTL; TTL; record review; renewed; deprecated record; review deprecated my record; fix a deprecated record; retire a record; grace period; record warns
OBJECTIVE: How capability records are reviewed on a TTL, and what to do when a review deprecates one of yours.

Consulted when a capability record's review comes due or a review deprecates one
of yours; return to the step that sent you.

**Keeping records current.** Every record has a TTL: when it passes, the record
is reviewed and either renewed or deprecated with the reason. When a review
deprecates one of yours, fix it (`update`) or retire it (`remove`). A
deprecated record still works and warns; it retires after its grace.
