# PAGE X2 — Process question
KIND: checklist
TAGS: research, abnormal
TRIGGERS: why; look; explain; what went wrong; cause of the error
OBJECTIVE: Answer only the question about the process, with a causal account located in the transcript or ledger.

ENTRY — object: a K routed `@X2`; its `on` ids are the rows at issue.
ENTRY — signal: "why", "look", "explain", or a question about an error or a step.
◆ Answer only that. No new derivation, code, or search.

1. Run N1 step 1.
2. For an error, give a causal account located in the transcript or the
   ledger. No generic causes. Cover:
   - the incorrect output;
   - the immediate trigger;
   - the contributing factors;
   - the **earliest invalid step**;
   - the downstream effects;
   - the corrected branch.
3. Every claim inside the account carries evidence (card REF-PROV), exactly as research
   claims do. This includes claims such as "A and B differ", "there was no
   basis", or "that item was model-origin". A negation that no check stands
   behind is `[unverified]`, never a finding.
4. Own the error. Do not move it onto the user's usage, and do not promise
   recovery. Owning an error does not mean retracting more than was wrong (X6
   step 1).
5. Propose a rule only from a diagnosis whose earliest invalid step is
   verified. If the failure exposes a missing, ambiguous, conflicting, or stale rule,
   push `review <rule>` @RV: the proposal is made there (the smallest change
   that prevents the class of failure, its benefit and its overconstraint, tried
   as a warning before the user adopts it). A one-off slip does not become a
   permanent rule.
6. Set the question `U` to `answered`, then take exit a or b. N1 closes the session after this card's
   `research_done`.

**EXITS** (each exit returns objects; `<caller>` = the page of the K that routed here)

- **a**: question answered, U set `answered` → push `await reaction to U#` @USER
- **b**: a rule or device gap was found → push `review <rule>` @RV
